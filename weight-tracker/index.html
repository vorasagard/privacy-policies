Privacy Policy – Weight Tracker

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Weight Tracker ("the app") is published by Sagar Vora ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app handles and the choices you have. Contact: vorasagard@gmail.com.

1. Information you enter, stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device. I do not receive it and it is not uploaded to any server I control:

  • Weight entries (weight, date, time, optional note)
  • Profile details: name, height, and optionally birth date and gender
  • Settings: unit (kg/lb), goal weight, theme

The app has no accounts and no cloud sync. This data is used to show your history, progress, BMI, goal progress, the home-screen widget and the "Log weight" shortcut. BMI and healthy-range figures are general information, not medical advice.

2. Export and import

You can export your data to a file you choose and import it back. The file is created where you save it; it is not sent to me. You are responsible for where you store or share it.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The app uses these services, which may collect data automatically:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google) – anonymous usage events (for example, which screens are opened and that a weight was logged) and device/app information, to understand and improve the app. Your weight values, name and other profile details are not sent as analytics events.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) – crash reports and diagnostics (device model, OS version, app state at the time of the crash) to find and fix bugs.
  • Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads SDK – displays ads. Google may collect the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions, and may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads.
  • Google User Messaging Platform – shows a consent form where required (for example, in the European Economic Area and the UK).

Their handling of data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Advertising choices

  • Where required, you can set or change your consent choices for ads in the consent form.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • You can turn off diagnostics and analytics sharing in the app's Settings, if that option is shown.

5. Sharing

I do not sell your personal information. Data from section 3 is processed by Google as described above. I do not share your weight or profile data with anyone, because I do not receive it.

6. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data: kept until you delete it. In Settings, "Delete all data" removes all entries, your goal and your profile and resets the app. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
  • Analytics and crash data: retained by Google under its retention policies. It is not linked to your name or profile. To ask about it, contact me at the address above.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

Data transmitted by the third-party services above is sent over encrypted connections. Data stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox and your device's own security (screen lock, device encryption).

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update the date above and publish the new version at this address.
